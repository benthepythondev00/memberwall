A Tribute alternative where your money never sits with the platform

Tribute is the best-known way to sell access on Telegram, and it is good at what it does. It collects your members' money first and pays it out to you later, on fixed dates and under its own rules. Memberwall never holds your money at all. Here is how the two differ, including where Tribute is the better choice.

When Tribute is the better choice

  • Your members expect to pay by card with one tap.
  • You also sell digital products, physical goods or take donations.
  • You want weekly or yearly subscriptions paid by card.

When Memberwall fits better

  • You want the money to reach you directly, with no payout dates and no account that can be blocked with your balance in it.
  • Your members pay in Stars, by bank transfer or with a local payment app.
  • You do not want to give up a percentage of every sale.
  • You are starting small and want something that costs nothing until it works.
