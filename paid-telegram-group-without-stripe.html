A paid Telegram group without Stripe: let members pay you directly

Stripe is not available in many countries, and many audiences pay with a local card app or a bank transfer. You can still run a paid group properly, without spreadsheets and without chasing people.

How most people do it today

A member sends the money and then a screenshot in a private message. You check it, add them to the group by hand and write down the date somewhere. A month later you have to remember who is due, write to them, and remove those who did not pay. It works with ten members and falls apart with a hundred.

The same thing, with the busywork removed

  1. You write one line for your members: the price and where to pay, the way you are paid today.
  2. A member opens your bot, sees that line, pays the usual way and sends the receipt to the bot as a photo or file.
  3. The receipt arrives in your chat with two buttons: Approve and Reject.
  4. When you approve, the bot sends the member a personal invite link that works once.
  5. The bot reminds the member 3 days and 1 day before their time runs out, and removes them when it ends unless a new receipt was approved.

What it does not do

  • It does not check the payment for you. You look at the receipt and decide.
  • It is not a card checkout. Nobody types a card number into the bot.
  • Your money never passes through Memberwall. It goes from your member to you, as it does today.

Setting it up

  1. Create a bot with @BotFather and send its token to @MemberwallBot.
  2. Add your bot to your group or channel as admin.
  3. Send /payinfo followed by the price and where to pay.
  4. Send /period to set how many days one payment covers, and /lang to choose the language your members see (English, Russian or Uzbek).

It is free for up to 5 approved payments every 30 days. Above that there are flat monthly plans. Members who prefer Telegram Stars can pay that way in the same bot.

Stuck at any step? Send /help and your question to @MemberwallBot. A person answers there and sets it up with you in writing, free for our first customers.
