How to create a paid Telegram channel with Telegram Stars

Telegram has its own way to charge for a channel: a paid invite link. Members pay in Telegram Stars every 30 days and Telegram lets them in. Here is how to set it up, what to know before you start, and where a bot helps.

The built-in way, step by step

  1. Make your channel private.
  2. Open the channel settings, go to the invite links and create a new link.
  3. Turn on the monthly fee and enter your price in Stars.
  4. Share that link. Anyone who opens it is asked to pay, and joins after paying.

The menu names differ a little between the phone and desktop apps, but the option sits with the invite links in every one of them.

What to know before you start

  • The period is always 30 days. There is no weekly, yearly or one-off option.
  • The Stars are credited to your channel, and you withdraw them through Telegram.
  • New Stars can take up to 21 days before you can withdraw them.
  • Members who buy Stars inside the phone app pay noticeably more than you receive, because the app stores take their share.
  • Telegram removes members whose subscription ends. You do not have to do it.

Where the built-in way stops

  • It works for channels only, not for groups.
  • Members can only pay in Stars. If your audience pays by bank card, a local payment app or PayPal, they are left out.
  • You get no simple overview of how many members you have and what they paid this month.

What Memberwall adds

Add @MemberwallBot to your channel as admin and send it your price. It creates the same paid link for you and keeps count: one message shows your members and the Stars they paid in the last 30 days.

For a group, or for members who cannot pay in Stars, you connect a bot of your own. Members then pay you directly, send the receipt to the bot, and you approve it with one tap. The bot sends each member a personal invite link, reminds them before their time runs out and removes them when it ends.
