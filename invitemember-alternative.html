An InviteMember alternative for small channels and groups

InviteMember has run paid Telegram communities for years and connects to more payment providers than anyone else. It has a free trial but no free plan, and its plans are tied to your sales. Here is how Memberwall differs, and when InviteMember is still the right call.

When InviteMember is the better choice

  • You want card payments to run automatically through Stripe or PayPal.
  • You need coupons, several projects or analytics.
  • You already sell enough that a yearly plan is an easy decision.

When Memberwall fits better

  • You are starting out and do not want to commit to a yearly plan first.
  • Your members pay in Stars, by bank transfer or with a local payment app.
  • You want to be running in ten minutes, without a dashboard to learn.
