Questions

Do my members need anything special?

Just Telegram. They tap your link and pay with Telegram Stars, or pay you directly (bank card, Payme, Click, PayPal) and send the receipt to the bot. Once the payment is confirmed they get a personal invite link.

Who holds the money?

You do. Stars go to the bot you created, and direct payments go to your own account. Memberwall never holds your funds.

What happens when someone stops paying?

They are reminded 3 days and 1 day before their time runs out, then removed automatically when it ends.

Is there a revenue share?

No. Plans are flat and depend only on how much your members pay you per 30 days. The Free plan costs nothing.

How do I get my money out?

You withdraw Stars through Telegram itself, the same way as for any bot. Telegram sets the rate and the waiting period.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Paid plans are a 30-day Telegram Stars subscription that you can cancel in Telegram whenever you like.
